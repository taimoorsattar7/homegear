## Overview

Noticing high user drop-offs, the team at Phobia came to the conclusion that they needed to improve their onboarding process.

Previously users selected their phobias manually, but this led to inaccurate profile tags. To address this, we designed an interactive onboarding experience that displays common visual scenarios and captures real user reactions.

The next phase is an interactive version of the onboarding flow where users are presented with a series of tailored scenarios to accurately calibrate their matching preferences.
