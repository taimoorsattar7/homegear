## Overview

Health records are fragmented across legacy systems. Unseal approached us to architect a decentralized, user-permissioned platform where patients securely own, manage, and share their verified health records.

We engineered high-throughput API endpoints, decentralized storage encryption, and seamless mobile wallet connectivity to allow users instant access to their health history anywhere in the world.
