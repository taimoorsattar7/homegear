## Objective

Cles Srl required a reliable digital platform to organize and present their independent consulting and research services. We developed a secure, accessible website to clearly communicate their expertise to public institutions, municipalities, and private enterprises.

## Technical Execution

We built this platform exclusively using custom WordPress development. We structured the architecture specifically for this CMS, intentionally avoiding frameworks like React, Next.js, Node.js, or PostgreSQL. We customized the backend so the Cles team can easily manage their news updates, client records, and service categories internally.

## Key Features

- **Custom WordPress Architecture:** Engineered a bespoke theme tailored directly to the firm's corporate identity and compliance standards.
- **Dynamic Content Management:** Configured a streamlined backend for fast updates to the news, events, and certifications sections.
- **Service Categorization:** Organized their specialized work areas—such as digital transition, welfare, and circular economy—into clear user paths.
- **Client Portfolio Display:** Built structured layouts to highlight their extensive track record with central administrations, regions, and municipalities.
- **Bilingual Infrastructure:** Integrated full Italian and English language toggles to expand their reach to international partners.
