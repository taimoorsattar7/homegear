## Objective

Gruppo CMB required a reliable, professional digital platform to present their 23 years of expertise in workplace safety and risk management. We developed a highly organized, responsive website to clearly communicate their consulting, training, and emergency surveillance services to large enterprise clients and public organizations.

## Technical Execution

We built this platform exclusively using custom WordPress development. We structured the backend to accommodate their specific CMS needs, strictly avoiding React, Next.js, Node.js, and PostgreSQL. We delivered a tailored administrative interface so the Gruppo CMB team can easily update their training course catalogs, news articles, and client testimonials without requiring technical intervention.

## Key Features

- **Custom WordPress Architecture:** Engineered a bespoke theme built directly around Gruppo CMB's branding and compliance standards.
- **Service Portfolio Integration:** Organized their three core pillars—Consulting, Training, and Fire Surveillance—into clear, accessible user pathways.
- **Dynamic Testimonial Management:** Implemented a structured layout to showcase reviews from high-profile clients like Wind Tre, Telethon, and IBL Banca to build immediate trust.
- **News & Regulatory Updates:** Configured a streamlined blogging engine for the client to publish regular updates on workplace safety regulations, contractor management, and high-risk training requirements.
- **Performance & Security Optimization:** Ensured fast load times and secure data handling to reflect the firm's focus on safety and reliability.
