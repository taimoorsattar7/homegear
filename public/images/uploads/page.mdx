Right Cam is a visual documentation and management tool that uses verifiable media to prove exactly what happens on the ground.

**Core Capabilities:**

- **Capture Verified Proof:** Every photo and video automatically locks in precise time, location, and context data the moment you hit record.
- **Search Instantly:** Filter your library of photos, videos, and documents by date, location, project, or team member.
- **Manage Work Live:** Monitor daily production and review project updates in real time.
- **Sync to the Cloud:** Sign in to automatically back up your files and keep your data accessible across devices.
- **Streamline Approvals:** Keep projects on schedule by tracking task progress and getting quick sign-offs from decision-makers.
